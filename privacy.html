Privacy Policy — Namaz Time

Last updated: 31 October 2026

Namaz Time ("the app") is developed by Sowrob Hasan Rana. This privacy policy explains how the app handles your information.

1. Data collection

Namaz Time does not collect, transmit, store, or share any personal data. The app works fully offline and has no user accounts, no sign-in, and no analytics.

2. Location

If you enable automatic location, the app uses your device's location only on your device to calculate accurate prayer times and the Qibla direction. Your location is never sent to any server or third party.

3. Device permissions

The app may request the following permissions, used only for the features described:

  • Location — to auto-detect your district for prayer-time calculation (optional; you can select your district manually instead).
  • Alarms / notifications — to play the azan alarm and prayer reminders at the times you choose.
  • Storage — to keep the offline Quran audio and app data on your device.

4. Third parties

The app contains no advertising SDKs, no analytics SDKs, and no third-party trackers. No data is shared with any third party.

5. Children's privacy

The app does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children under 13, because it collects no data at all.

6. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this same URL with a revised "Last updated" date.
